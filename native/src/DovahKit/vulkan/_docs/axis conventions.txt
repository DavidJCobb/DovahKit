
Euler rotations have a "handedness," which determines whether increasing angles 
turn an object clockwise  (lefthanded) or counterclockwise  (righthanded). This 
property only applies to rotations, not to positions.

Vulkan clip space is  righthanded. However, Skyrim's  game data uses lefthanded 
intrinsic XYZ rotations; increasing angles turn an object clockwise.

 - Swapping any two rotation angles (e.g. std::swap(x, y)) will flip handedness 
   as a side effect.

 - Negating any one angle will flip handedness  as a side effect. However, this 
   also changes the rotation itself --  the flipped angle's relationship to the 
   other two angles, and to the position axes.

 - Negating two angles does not change handedness,  and is equivalent to a 180-
   degree rotation about those two angles.

 - Negating all three angles changes  handedness with no other apparent effect, 
   and is therefore the ideal approach for our situation.
